Logo

The official SVG from the live site, used unmodified: dark-teal bars #143c46 and a coral full stop #f55a5a. Top-left at 28–36 px, with clear space at least the width of the dot. Never recoloured or redrawn. Always shown alongside "Independent concept · not Lilli".
