## Limitations
- **The family product is waitlisted.** I couldn't install or try it, so this concept doesn't describe how Lilli's onboarding, app or data actually behave.
- No user research has been done. There are no interviews, quotes or survey results anywhere in this repo.
- No Lilli data. Every household, sensor event, time, count and funnel number is synthetic.
- No medical or clinical validation. The product deliberately makes no health claims.
- Lilli's public homepage statistics are Lilli's own claims and are not reused here.
