# Role viability memo

**Role:** Product Manager, Lilli (public listing: https://apply.workable.com/lilli/j/4CEB76F630/). The listing describes owning the Family product area: proposition, onboarding, regular use, engagement and retention, alongside discovery, analytics and experiments, where trust is essential.

## Why this concept is relevant to the role
- **Proposition and onboarding:** The concept treats the first week as the product. Consent, setup and first insight are designed as one journey with a clear "first useful insight" definition.
- **Engagement and retention:** I define weekly return against a denominator that excludes revocations, so retention work never pushes against someone's right to stop sharing.
- **Analytics:** There is an event catalogue with properties, exclusions and guardrails, plus a forbidden-property guard, so measurement can't leak identity.
- **Experiments:** Two pre-registered designs with guardrails that can stop a winning variant.
- **Trust:** Honest-gap rules, no diagnosis, a no-bypass consent engine and calm notification defaults.

## What I don't know (and would learn first)
- How the real family onboarding works today. It is waitlisted publicly, so I couldn't try it.
- Real funnel baselines, device reliability, support contact drivers and who actually buys.
- Constraints from the existing B2B/council product, the data platform and clinical governance.

## First 30/60/90 days I'd propose
- **30:** Shadow support and sales, read existing research, run the discovery guide, and map the real first-week funnel with its true denominators.
- **60:** Instrument M1–M4 and G1–G4; fix the biggest honest-gap problem; set up E1.
- **90:** Run E1, prepare E2, and agree a trust scorecard (revocation, misunderstanding, stale reassurance) alongside growth metrics.

## Limits of this memo
It is based only on public pages. It makes no claim about Lilli's internal metrics, roadmap or problems.
