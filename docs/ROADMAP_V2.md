# V2 roadmap (concept)

Ordered by trust first, then value. Each item needs discovery before build.

1. **Shared family circle:** invite siblings with the monitored person's approval, one summary, and role-based visibility.
2. **Monitored person's own view:** a simple weekly "here's what your family sees" card, so sharing feels mutual.
3. **Weekday/weekend baselines:** after 3–4 weeks, compare like with like and shrink false "looks different" flags.
4. **Coverage repair nudges:** guided fixes for a specific offline sensor (battery, plug, hub), sent to whoever placed it.
5. **Pause, not revoke:** a holiday or visitor pause that keeps consent but stops summaries (avoids false flags).
6. **Accessibility modes:** large-text and read-aloud versions of the consent screen; an assisted-consent conversation guide.
7. **Retention and deletion controls:** user-visible data retention and a "delete my history" option for the monitored person.
8. **Out of scope until legal and safeguarding design exists:** consent on behalf of someone without capacity, and any health inference.
