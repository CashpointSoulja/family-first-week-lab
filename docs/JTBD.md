# Jobs to be done

These are hypotheses from public information and general reasoning. They are not drawn from interviews; see [DISCOVERY_GUIDE.md](DISCOVERY_GUIDE.md) for how I'd test them.

## Family member (buyer)
- **Main job:** When I can't be there every day, I want a light, trustworthy sign that the usual routine has started, so I can stop worrying without intruding.
- **Related jobs:** Notice when the routine looks different early enough to just ask about it. Share the load with siblings without relaying updates.
- **Emotional job:** Feel like a good son, daughter or friend, not a surveillance operator.
- **Hiring criteria:** Easy to set up, doesn't nag, tells me when it doesn't know.
- **Firing triggers (hypothesised):** A false alarm at 2am, a cheerful summary on a day the hub was offline, or the relationship feeling worse.

## Monitored person
- **Main job:** When my family worries, I want to give them enough reassurance to back off, while keeping my privacy and independence.
- **Hiring criteria:** I choose what is shared, no cameras or microphones, I can stop at any time.
- **Firing triggers (hypothesised):** Feeling watched or questioned about the data, or something shared that they didn't expect.

## How the prototype responds
| Job | Prototype answer |
|---|---|
| Light sign the day has started | First insight: "Kettle first used around 07:40", with freshness |
| Notice change, not diagnose | Weekly "looks different" with counts and a check-in suggestion |
| Share the load | Goal option "Share check-ins with other family" (one summary) |
| Privacy and control | Per-category consent, revoke per area or all, "Not shared" shown to family |
| Don't nag | Weekly digest by default; no real-time alerts |
