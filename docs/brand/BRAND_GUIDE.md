# Visual brand guide (concept use only)

Sampled from Lilli's public website CSS and imagery for an independent concept. The logo and brand belong to Intelligent Lilli; I use them only to show product judgement in an application.

## Logo
- Official SVG from the live site (`lilli-logo-colourfix.svg`), saved unmodified at `public/brand/lilli-logo.svg`: five dark-teal bars (#143c46) and a coral full stop (#f55a5a).
- Top-left, 28–36 px tall, with clear space of at least the coral dot's diameter. Never recoloured, stretched or redrawn.
- Always paired with the label "Independent concept by Ayo Ahmed · Not affiliated with Lilli".

## Shape and components
- Cards: 24 px radius (site sections use 24–32 px); app cards 16 px radius on white.
- CTA: outline pill with a long arrow (⟶); primary dark pill like the site's "Login".
- App motifs from Lilli's public mockups: the large pale-teal person circle with a house icon, small stat circles, a cream date band, and per-sensor dot rows.
- Status pills: icon plus word (never colour alone): ✓ Ready, ◔ Waiting, ✕ Offline, – Not shared, ! Stale.

## Imagery
No real people's photos: the concept uses abstract house icons and initials. Reference screenshots of the public site are kept in `docs/brand/` for comparison only.
