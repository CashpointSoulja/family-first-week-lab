# ELI5

Imagine your gran lives on her own and you live far away. You'd like to know her day has started as usual, without phoning her every morning.

Little sensors can notice things like "the kettle was used" or "the front door opened". They can't see or hear her.

This prototype is about the first week:

1. **Ask Gran first.** She picks which sensors you can see. She can say no, and she can change her mind later. You can't say yes for her.
2. **Put the sensors up.** The app shows which ones are working. A sensor that isn't working means "we don't know", not "everything is fine".
3. **First note after a full day.** For example, "Kettle first used around 7:40". It also tells you how fresh the data is and how sure it is.
4. **Weekly summary.** "Her week looked similar" or "fewer front-door trips in the last three days". It never says why, because sensors can't know that. If something looks different, the suggestion is just to give her a call.
5. **Lab.** A page for product people with ideas to test and made-up numbers. None of it comes from Lilli.
